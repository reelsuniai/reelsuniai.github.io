# Reel rules (ShortsUniversity): apply to every video

These come from the insights on the "11 free Claude skills" reel (42 s): 64% skip rate, and most viewers gone by second 3.
Engagement from the people who stayed was good (saves 5.0%, shares 2.9%, comments 2.4%), so the content works; the opening and length don't.

## Length
- **Under 30 s**, aim for 18–25 s. Voice at a natural, punchy pace (~3 words/s).
- One idea per sentence. Cut every word that isn't value: no "Someone built…", "Here's what I'd use first", or wind-up.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
- The **first sentence says the payoff** and is under 3 s spoken (≤ 9 words).
- The **first frame already shows the payoff**: big, bold, full-screen. No empty frame, nothing tiny, no slow drop-in.
- Something visibly moves in frame 0 (a pop, a counter, a stamp). A visual change at least every ~0.5 s through second 3.

## Middle
- Each point = **one line, one big visual** that shows the actual result. Keep beats ~3 s; cut on the word.
- Big elements (cards fill the width, readable at phone size). No small UI.
- Never a flat stretch over ~1.5 s.

## Ending
- CTA is one short line ("Comment WORD for the link."), ≤ 2.5 s.

## Always
- Credit the creator when sharing someone else's free tool.
- No invented stats about real products. Example numbers on screen are illustrations only.
