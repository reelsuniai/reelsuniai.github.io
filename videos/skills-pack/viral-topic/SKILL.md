# /viral-topic

Goal: 10 topics people already watch, each rewritten as the user's own angle. Never invent view counts.

1. Ask (once, only if missing): niche, audience, Shorts or long, language.
2. List 10 topic patterns that repeatedly perform in this niche (formats like "I let X do Y for 7 days", "N things nobody tells you about X", "Stop doing X", "How X actually makes money"). If you can browse, check that several creators made it; otherwise say "pattern, not verified".
3. For each topic give:
   - **Proven pattern** (one line)
   - **Your angle**: the user's version in one sentence, specific and first-person
   - **Hook** under 9 words
   - **Payoff**: what the viewer gets at the end
4. Rank the top 3 by (easy to make × curiosity × fit with the user's offer) and say which one to film first and why.

Output as a numbered list. No fluff, no intro paragraph.
