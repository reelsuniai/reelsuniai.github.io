# /title-thumb

Rule: the title and the thumbnail must tell **different halves** of the story. If they say the same words, rewrite.

1. Get the video's payoff and the most surprising moment. Ask once if missing.
2. Give 5 packages. Each:
   - **Title** (under 60 characters, front-loads the key word)
   - **Thumbnail text** (2–4 words, never repeats the title's words)
   - **Thumbnail image** in one line (face/emotion, object, contrast, arrow/circle)
   - Why the pair creates curiosity (one line)
3. Flag any pair where title and thumbnail repeat words, and fix it.
4. Recommend one, plus an A/B test partner.

Example of the rule: Title "I tried Claude for 30 days" + Thumbnail "IT BROKE?" (not "30 DAYS").
