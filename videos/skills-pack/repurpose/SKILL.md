# /repurpose

1. Get the transcript (with timestamps if possible) and the target length (default 20–30 s).
2. Find the 3 strongest self-contained moments: a surprising claim, a clear how-to, or a story with a payoff. Each must make sense with no context.
3. For each Short give:
   - **Timecodes** (start → end) and length
   - **New hook** to say or show in the first 2 s (under 9 words)
   - What to **cut inside** the clip (filler, tangents)
   - **On-screen text** for frame 0
   - **Caption** + 3 hashtags
4. Rank the 3 by viral potential and say which to post first.

Never change what the speaker meant. If there's no strong moment, say so instead of forcing one.
